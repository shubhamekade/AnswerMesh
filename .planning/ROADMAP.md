## Milestone 4: WooCommerce & Agency Engine (Phase 11)

### Phase 11: WooCommerce Structured Commerce & Agency Engine
- **Goal:** Provide rich e-commerce entity schema, buying guides, product question answering, and white-label multi-site agency management.
- **Deliverables:**
  - WooCommerce JSON-LD Schema (`Product`, `Offer`, `AggregateRating`, `MerchantReturnPolicy`).
  - Automated product question generation & buying guide generator.
  - Multi-site / network-level settings synchronization.
  - White-label branding customization and centralized agency reporting export.
- **Plans:**
  - `11-01`: WooCommerce Schema & Product Question Extraction Engine
  - `11-02`: Autonomous Buying Guide & Comparison Generator
  - `11-03`: Multi-Site Management & Agency White-Label Hub
