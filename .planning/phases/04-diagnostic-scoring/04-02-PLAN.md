# Plan 04-02: Composite Visibility Score & Rating Engine

**Phase:** 4 — Multi-Layer Diagnostic Scoring System  
**Plan ID:** 04-02  
**Type:** Core Feature Subsystem  
**Requirements Addressed:** REQ-F16, NFR-01, NFR-02  
**Status:** Planned  

---

## 1. Objective
Calculate the overall composite AnswerMesh Visibility Score from the 6 sub-scores, map to qualitative diagnostic levels (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent), and persist scores to `wp_aiseo_scores` for posts and site-wide rollups.

---

## 2. Context & Design Decisions
- **Weighting Formula:**
  - `overall = (seo * 0.20) + (aeo * 0.20) + (geo * 0.20) + (entity * 0.15) + (trust * 0.15) + (content * 0.10)`
- **Rating Labels:**
  - 85–100: `Excellent`
  - 70–84: `Good`
  - 50–69: `Fair`
  - 0–49: `Needs Attention`
- **Database Persistence:**
  - Save to `{$wpdb->prefix}aiseo_scores` with post_id (0 for site rollup, >0 for individual post).
  - Also update post meta `_aiseo_score` for lightweight post list column queries.

---

## 3. Detailed Tasks

### Task 1: Overall Score Calculation & Rating Mapper

  - `get_rating_label(int $score): string`
  - `compute_and_save_post_score(int $post_id): array`
  - `compute_and_save_site_score(): array`
