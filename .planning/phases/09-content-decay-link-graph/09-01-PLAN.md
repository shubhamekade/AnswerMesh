---

## 1. Objective
Build the content freshness and decay evaluation engine `DecayEngine` (`includes/class-decay-engine.php`). Calculate content decay based on publication age, modification date, entity stability, and scoring trends, categorizing posts into `Fresh`, `Aging`, `Decaying`, and `Critical`. Connect decay findings to the Evidence Model recommendation engine.

---

## 2. Context & Design Decisions
- **Decay Formula & Tiers:**
  - Days since modified:
    - 0–60 days: `Fresh` (Score 90–100)
    - 61–180 days: `Aging` (Score 70–89)
    - 181–365 days: `Decaying` (Score 40–69)
    - 365+ days: `Critical` (Score 0–39)
  - Bonus/Penalty adjustments:
    - Active schema presence & recent entity updates: +10 freshness.
    - High diagnostic visibility score: +10 freshness.
    - Broken/outdated factual references: -20 freshness.
- **Evidence Model Integration:**
  - For posts in `Decaying` or `Critical` tier, emit `content_decay_alert` recommendations:
    - Priority `CRITICAL` if >365 days unedited and overall score <60.
    - Priority `HIGH` if 180–365 days unedited.
- **REST Endpoints:**
  - `GET /aiseo/v1/decay/inventory`: List all posts with freshness score, tier badge, days since modified, and priority.
  - `POST /aiseo/v1/decay/recalculate`: Recalculate site-wide freshness metrics.
