## 1. Objective
Develop the sitewide internal hyperlink parser and graph builder `LinkGraph` (`includes/class-link-graph.php`). Calculate in-degree, out-degree, link isolation / true orphan pages, anchor text distribution, and scan contextual candidate text across articles to discover high-value internal linking opportunities between cluster members and pillar pages.

---

## 2. Context & Design Decisions
- **Internal Hyperlink Extraction:**
  - Scans `post_content` of all published posts for `<a\s+[^>]*href=["']([^"']+)["'][^>]*>(.*?)<\/a>`.
  - Normalizes URLs, matches against `home_url()`, and maps internal URLs to target post IDs using `url_to_postid()`.
  - Captures edge metadata: `source_post_id`, `target_post_id`, `anchor_text`, `is_external`, `rel`.
- **Graph Metrics:**
  - `in_degree`: Number of inbound internal links to a post.
  - `out_degree`: Number of outbound internal links from a post.
  - `orphan_pages`: Published posts with `in_degree === 0` (zero inbound links).
  - `anchor_diversity`: Unique anchors used to link to target.
- **Smart Anchor Opportunity Finder:**
  - Inspects target post title, primary entity names, and cluster subtopics.
  - Searches published posts in the same or related cluster for occurrences of those phrases that are not already inside an `<a>` tag.
  - Extracts 30-word contextual snippet around the phrase.
  - Emits recommendation finding with proposed anchor, target URL, and sentence context.
- **REST Endpoints:**
  - `GET /aiseo/v1/links/graph`: Full graph summary (total internal links, node stats, in-degree distribution).
  - `GET /aiseo/v1/links/orphans`: Posts with 0 inbound links.
  - `GET /aiseo/v1/links/opportunities`: Suggested link opportunities for current post or sitewide.
